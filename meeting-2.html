CMS Health Tech Ecosystem · Diagnostic Imaging WorkgroupSuccess criteria

What success looks like

1 · Operations · 27.3%

Stop routine disk use

  • Stop buying and maintaining burners.
  • Keep only a small downtime process.
  • Remove routine disc queues.
2 · Clinical workflow · 18.2%

Get priors before the visit

  • Retrieve outside MRIs electronically.
  • Avoid phone, fax, and manual chasing.
  • Ingest images into clinical workflows.
3 · Patients / apps · 36.4%

Give patients the real study

  • Full-fidelity DICOM in an app they choose.
  • Portable images across health systems.

Direct / early implementation

Use authorization paths that work today

  • Patient SMART authorization
  • Bilateral backend client
  • Local clinician workflow
  • Site-specific onboarding where needed
Network-facilitated / scale

Use the network as the control plane

  • Establish participant trust
  • Locate responding sites, endpoints, and records
  • Support client registration / onboarding
  • Responding organization authorizes access to its API
